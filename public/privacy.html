Privacy Policy

Eggers Kick Off · by Click Wise Design, Allen, Texas · Effective 16 October 2026

Eggers Kick Off is a free arcade kicking game. We collect as little as we can: just what's needed to save your progress, run the leaderboards and multiplayer, and keep the game friendly. We do not show ads, sell data, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or payment details.

Stored on your device

The game stores your offline profile, settings, the people you've blocked, your login code and a signed backup of your online progress in your browser or app storage, so the game works offline and your progress can't be lost. Clearing the app's data or your browser's site data removes it.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except the services that run the game for us:

  • Railway (railway.com) hosts the game server and database in the United States.
  • Google Fonts serves the game's fonts, so your device fetches them from Google, which sees your IP address as part of that request.

Your kicker name, level, scores and replays are public to other players on the leaderboards. We may disclose data if required by law.

Deleting your data

In the game: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your online profile, progress, rounds, replays, leaderboard entries and login codes are deleted from our database immediately, and the backup on that device is erased. This can't be undone.

Without the app: contact us through clickwisedesign.com with your kicker name and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Reports about you that were already filed are kept for up to 12 months for safety, then deleted. Server backups held by our host roll off within 30 days.

Keeping the game friendly

Names and chat are filtered for offensive words. You can block any player (hides their chat and posts on your device) and report them from the leaderboard, a room, or a chat message. We review reports and may rename, mute or ban accounts that break the rules.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone, but online profiles and multiplayer chat are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online profile, contact us and we'll delete it. The offline modes need no account.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with scrypt, login codes are stored only as hashes, and progress backups are tamper-proof (signed).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or US state privacy laws) you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data. You can rename or delete your profile yourself in the game, or contact us for anything else.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and mention it in the in-game patch notes.
