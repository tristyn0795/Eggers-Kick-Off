How to play

  1. Watch Jason juggle. The ball pops off his knee to a different height every time.
  2. Click, tap or press any key to swing. His foot meets the ball a split-second later, so time it early.
  3. Strike in the green sweet spot (chest–head height) for a PERFECT kick. A great first bounce lands around 900 m.
  4. Too high and Jason whiffs. Hitting it low sends a weak line drive.
  5. Bounce pads launch the ball further. Puddles stop it dead. Watch the wind.
  6. Power Balls: every 5 PERFECT kicks in a row load a special next ball: 🔥 Fireball (+35% distance), 🏀 Super Bouncy (+25%, huge bounces), ☄️ Meteor (+45%, no bounce: it craters), 🧲 Magnet (pulls a nearby stop into the bullseye) or ✂️ Split Shot (splits in two, and both balls score).
  7. 🪅 Piñata Ball: keep it going to 9 PERFECTs in a row and the next ball is a Piñata. It flies your full distance, cracks open where it lands and drops a prize: a pile of coins, a free upgrade level, a +5 upgrade boost for your next 2 rounds, or a new outfit, ball or trail.

Scoring — Topgolf style

  • Every 10 m the ball travels = 1 point.
  • Stop the ball inside a target for bonus points: outer ring ×1, inner ring ×2, bullseye ×3.
  • The Click Wise Design Office sits at 4,000 m (250 pts). The field keeps going: Downtown, the Airport, the Moon Base, the Pyramids, the Black Hole and the Eggers Galaxy HQ at 68 km… then a Frontier Beacon every 8 km.
  • A round is 10 kicks, and the last ball scores double.

🪙 How coins work

Progression

  • Every round's points also count as XP. Level up to unlock new gear and higher upgrade levels.
  • Spend coins on upgrades (power, bounce, sweet spot, slow-mo…) up to level 50, and on outfits, balls and trails.
  • Prestige ⭐: once your upgrades total 150 levels, reset them for a permanent +10% coins and +2% kick power each time, plus prestige-only gear.
  • Career: beat bosses to raise your Skill Level. Reach 1,000 to go PRO and earn the Mastery Pro Ball.
  • Online progress is saved to your kicker name. Offline modes keep their own local progress on this device.

Play nice
